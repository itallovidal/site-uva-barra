## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Image gallery slider section

The Sobre page SHALL display an image gallery slider section with UVA campus photos.

#### Scenario: Slider renders with UVA images

- **WHEN** the sobre page loads
- **THEN** the page SHALL display a slider section containing the 4 UVA campus images from `public/uva/uva-1.jpg` through `public/uva/uva-4.jpg`
- **AND** each slide SHALL display the image with proper aspect ratio
- **AND** the slider SHALL support arrow navigation and dot indicators
